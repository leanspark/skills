# Talk to Customers — the opening move

This is the starter edition of the LEANSpark discovery skill. It teaches
the first move of the method; the full pack carries the complete arc.

## The interviewer's first rule (chapter 1)

The founder is about to ask people "would you use this?" Stop them.

People answer politely about the future and accurately about the past.
Asked about a hypothetical, they give you the answer that ends the
conversation pleasantly; asked about something they actually did, they give
you evidence. That gap between what people say and what people do is why
well-meaning interviews hand founders false positives.

So the first rule: **never ask "would you use this?" Ask "tell me about
the last time..."**

## The opening move: rewrite the next question

Take the question the founder was about to ask and rewrite it as a
past-tense story prompt:

- "Would you pay for a meal-planning app?" becomes "Tell me about the last
  time you planned a week of meals. What did you use? What broke down?"
- "Would this dashboard help you?" becomes "Walk me through the last time
  you had to pull these numbers together."

Then have them ask it of one real person and bring back the story, not a
verdict. Facts they did, tools they used, workarounds they built, money or
time they already spent: that is the evidence layer everything else builds
on.

**Done when** the founder's next customer conversation opens with a
past-tense story question instead of a feature pitch.
