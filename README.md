# LEANSpark Playbook Skills

Running Lean, in your agent's vocabulary.

LEANSpark (leanspark.ai) is LEANSTACK's AI product for practicing Running
Lean: Lean Canvas, customer interviews, and 90-day validation cycles. This
marketplace carries the **starter editions** of the LEANSpark Playbook
Skills — each teaches your agent the opening move of a Running Lean
playbook, readable by humans and runnable by agents.

## Install

In Claude Code:

```
/plugin marketplace add leanspark/skills
/plugin install leanspark@leanspark-skills
```

The skills then appear in `/skills` and fire on their own when a
conversation calls for them.

## The skills

| Skill | The job | From the book |
|---|---|---|
| `leanspark:stress-test` | Share a startup idea; get it drafted onto a one-page Lean Canvas, marked as yours to correct | *Is Your Idea Worth Pursuing* |
| `leanspark:interview` | Start customer discovery: who to talk to, and why you can't trust what customers say | *How to Talk to Customers* |

These are starter editions — the opening move of each playbook. The **full
packs** carry the complete choreographies (the four-gate thought test,
variant splits, viability math, three-act interviews, Customer Forces
synthesis) and are a signed-in download at
[leanspark.ai/skill-packs](https://leanspark.ai/skill-packs?src=marketplace).
The scored stress test and interview lab run in your LEANSpark workspace.

Pair the skills with the **LEANSpark MCP connector**
(`https://leanspark.ai/mcp`, OAuth) so your agent reads your live business
model instead of starting from a blank page:
[leanspark.ai/mcp-server](https://leanspark.ai/mcp-server).

More at [leanspark.ai/skills](https://leanspark.ai/skills).
